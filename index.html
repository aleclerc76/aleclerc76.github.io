Welcome

I am Augustin Leclerc and I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in Applied Mathematics in CONCACE team of INRIA, Airbus and Cerfacs, in France. I am working on the construction of reduced systems for numerical methods applied to physical problems, such as wave propagation in structures containing thin slots.

Before my postdoctoral fellowship, I was a PhD student in Applied Mathematics in the LMI of INSA Rouen Normandie and the Makutu team at Inria Bordeaux–Sud-Ouest, directed by Christian Gout (LMI), Hélène Barucq (Makutu) and Antoine Tonnoir (LMI). I was focusing on mathematical modelling for the computation of guided electromagnetic modes in open and twisted waveguides (please visit this page to consult my thesis).
